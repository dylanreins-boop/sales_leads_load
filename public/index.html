                      Import card payments

                      Card payments from Talech / Elavon (or any card processor), matched to the jobs they paid for.

                      In your Talech back office (or Elavon's Converge / Merchant Connect), open the transactions or sales report for the days you want, export it to Excel or CSV, and choose the file here. The app matches each card payment to the job it paid for by amount, customer name and date. Check the matches, then record them. Declines, voids and cash are left out, and a payment already recorded is never added twice, so it's fine to upload overlapping dates.
